# Planwrit — Quick Ad VERSION A (Split Test)
## Hook: "Most business plans answer the wrong question."

**Version:** 1.0  
**Format:** 16:9 (YouTube) + export crop for 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Shorts)  
**Length:** 30 seconds
**Split Test Version:** A  
**Style:** Bold text-driven + stock footage + AI voiceover (optional)  
**Production time:** 2–4 hours in Canva or CapCut  
**Cost:** Free (stock footage from Pexels, editing in Canva/CapCut)

---

## CONCEPT

No face. No recording. The story is told through bold text overlays and stock footage. 
This style performs well as a YouTube pre-roll, Instagram Reel, and Facebook ad 
because it works with the sound OFF — many viewers watch without audio.

---

## FULL SCRIPT — SCENE BY SCENE

---

### SCENE 1 — HOOK
**Timing:** 0–4 sec  
**Stock footage:** Close-up of hands reviewing official-looking documents / visa paperwork  
**Music:** Starts quietly — subtle cinematic underscore  

**TEXT ON SCREEN (large, centred, white on dark overlay):**
```
Most business plans answer
the wrong question.
```

**TEXT ANIMATION:** Line 1 fades in, line 2 appears 0.5 sec later  
**No voiceover — silence + text is the hook**

---

### SCENE 2 — AGITATE THE PROBLEM
**Timing:** 4–8 sec  
**Stock footage:** Person sitting at desk looking stressed / frustrated at laptop  

**TEXT ON SCREEN:**

We build plans that do.
